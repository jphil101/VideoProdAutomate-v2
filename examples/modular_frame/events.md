# Events: extending a modular steel frame by one bay

Site: a one-storey modular steel frame with 2 bays along X at 6 m spacing (gridlines A, B, C)
and 1 bay along Y at 7.5 m (gridlines 1 and 2). Storey height 4 m. A tower crane serves the site.

1. Footings and the six UC 305x305 columns at A1, B1, C1, A2, B2 and C2 are already standing.
2. Beams PB-1 (A1–B1) and PB-3 (A2–B2), both UB 406x178, already frame the first bay.
3. Precast slab S-1 already covers bay A1–B2.
4. Primary beam PB-2, an identical UB 406x178, is lifted from the laydown yard and placed between columns B1 and C1 at level 1, extending the 6 m module along gridline 1.
5. Its twin, beam PB-4, is placed between B2 and C2 on gridline 2.
6. The new beam-to-column connections are bolted.
7. Precast hollow-core slab S-2 drops into bay B1–C2, completing a second module identical to the first.
